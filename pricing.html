FAQs

Pricing Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about how we charge for rides.

Is there any surge pricing during peak hours?
No. Local Taxi operates on fixed pricing. Rates do not change during rush hours, weekends, or holidays. The only additional charge is a 15% night surcharge for rides between 10 PM and 6 AM.
How is the fare calculated if I change my destination mid-ride?
If you change your destination during the ride, the fare will be recalculated automatically based on the actual distance traveled to the new destination using the GPS-tracked route.
Are there any hidden charges I should know about?
Absolutely not. The only additional charges are: Airport surcharge, Night surcharge (10PMâ€“6AM), and a Cancellation fee if you cancel after the driver is dispatched. All charges are shown upfront before you confirm your booking.
Can I pay with cash or only cards?
We accept both cash and digital payments. You can pay by credit/debit card, mobile wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), or cash directly to the driver. Your receipt will always be sent digitally.
Is there a discount for corporate accounts or frequent riders?
Yes! We offer corporate accounts with monthly invoicing and preferential rates. Frequent riders also earn loyalty credits on every ride. Contact us at corporate@localtaxi.com to set up your account.
What is the cancellation policy?
You can cancel for free within 2 minutes of booking. After that, a small cancellation fee applies (Economy: $2, Premium: $3, SUV: $4) if the driver has already been dispatched to your location.
