Who We Are

More Than a Taxi Company

Local Taxi was founded in 2016 by two engineers who were tired of unreliable rides, surge pricing, and poor driver standards. They believed city transportation could be better — safer, more comfortable, and completely transparent.

What started as a small fleet of 12 vehicles in New York has grown into a premium urban mobility platform operating across 32+ cities, completing over 1.2 million rides, and trusted by Fortune 500 companies for corporate travel.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These aren't just words on a wall — they drive every decision we make, every ride we dispatch, and every driver we partner with.

Safety First, Always

Every driver is background-checked, licensed, and re-evaluated quarterly. Every vehicle is inspected monthly. Safety is never negotiable.

Radical Transparency

Fixed fares, no hidden fees, no surge pricing. What you see is what you pay — full stop. We believe in earning trust through honesty.

Premium Experience

Every touchpoint matters — from the booking UI to the driver's greeting. We sweat the small details so our riders don't have to.

Inclusive Mobility

Premium transportation for everyone — regardless of ability, age, or background. Accessible vehicles and inclusive design are core to our mission.

Environmental Responsibility

30% of our fleet is now electric or hybrid. We're on track for a fully electric fleet by 2030, reducing our carbon footprint ride by ride.

Driver Partnership

Our drivers are partners, not contractors. We offer fair pay, health benefits, and continuous training. Happy drivers mean happy riders.

Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

2016
Founded in New York City

Started with 12 vehicles and a team of 5. First 100 rides completed in the first week.

2018
Expanded to 5 Cities

Launched corporate accounts and grew to 150 vehicles across NYC, Boston, Chicago, Miami, and LA.

2020
Launched Electric Fleet

Introduced 50 Tesla Model 3s and Model Ys. Committed to full electric fleet by 2030.

2022
1 Million Rides Milestone

Crossed 1 million completed rides. Expanded to 20 cities with 350+ vehicles in service.

2026
Today & Beyond

Operating in 32+ cities with 500+ vehicles. Working towards international expansion and a fully green fleet.
